# Research finding: The Depth of the Landscape

## Current editorial decision

Publish `RM → The Depth of the Landscape` as both `mentioned` and `read`. Keep `recommended` in research until the wording of RM’s original 2020-10-04 Weverse post is checked directly.

## Book evidence

The official Weverse Magazine feature [Getting to know Korean modern and contemporary art with RM](https://magazine.weverse.io/article/view/120?artist=BTS&lang=en) says that RM had been reading *The Depth of Landscape*, a collection of Kang Yobae’s art and personal writing. This supports a verified `read` relation, not merely a visible book or generic mention.

A contemporaneous [Koreaboo article](https://www.koreaboo.com/news/bts-rm-latest-book-film-song-recommendations/) reproduces images from RM’s 2020-10-04 Weverse post and attributes an explicit recommendation of the book, documentary and song to him. The exact legacy [Weverse URL](https://weverse.io/bts/artist/1640184234924405) was recovered from a contemporaneous [r/bangtan weekly archive](https://www.reddit.com/r/bangtan/comments/j5032t). The current post page requires an authenticated community session, so `recommended` remains an open primary-source check.

## Cross-domain art path

This book produces a strong bridge from Literature into Visual Art and Museums & Exhibitions:

```text
RM
├── read → The Depth of the Landscape
│   └── created by → Kang Yobae
└── visited → Kang Yobae: Carnation — When Mind Becomes Body
    └── shown at → Daegu Art Museum
        └── featured work → Munnamu (2020)
```

In its [institutional Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/p/CWCNRdyvJDA/), Daegu Art Museum states that RM visited its exhibitions and left a verification photo with Kang Yobae’s *Munnamu* (`먼나무`, 2020). The museum supplies the artwork title, artist, year, medium and dimensions. The exhibition [Kang Yobae: Carnation — When Mind Becomes Body](https://www.artbava.com/exhibits/%EA%B0%95%EC%9A%94%EB%B0%B0-%EC%B9%B4%EB%84%A4%EC%9D%B4%EC%85%98-%EB%A7%88%EC%9D%8C%EC%9D%B4-%EB%AA%B8%EC%9D%B4-%EB%90%A0-%EB%95%8C) ran at the museum from 2021-10-13 to 2022-01-09.

The museum evidence supports `visited` and `mentioned`. It does not establish that RM owned or collected the artwork, and it does not by itself justify the more subjective relation `admired`.

## Publication-safe wording now

> Weverse Magazine says RM was reading Kang Yobae’s *The Depth of the Landscape*. In 2021, Daegu Art Museum also confirmed that RM visited its exhibitions and posted a photograph with Kang’s *Munnamu*, creating a documented path from the artist’s writing to his exhibited work.
